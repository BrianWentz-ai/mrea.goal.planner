Where the planning assumptions come from

This planner adapts economic-model concepts from The Millionaire Real Estate Agent (Keller, Jenks & Papasan, 2004). Historical book examples are used only as editable starting points; they are not current industry averages, promises, or required spending levels.

Cost of Sales and Fixed Operating Expenses are separated so the model stays congruent with a real estate profit-and-loss structure. Expense categories are pre-populated as planning benchmarks to support production without allowing overhead to consume profitability; every amount is editable.

The default seller and buyer conversion assumptions are planning examples. Replace them with your actual conversion rates as soon as you have reliable data. Working weeks default to 47, reserving five weeks away from regular production.

The Monthly Four Conversations scorecard shows goals for appointments, listings taken and buyer commitments, closed units, and GCI. Monthly counts round up separately for each side. Actual-results tracking is a future step. Estimated sale prices are automatically linked to average GCI per deal using an internal planning assumption; estimated volume is for reference and is not a market valuation.

Personal taxes are already included in the saved Life by Design income target and are not added again as business expenses.
