STEP 5 / YOUR WEEKLY BUSINESS ROUTINE

Make room for the business—and life—you want.

Start with your suggested weekly rhythm. Move or resize blocks, add activities from the tray, or open Customize My Weekly Schedule for detailed settings.

1. Protect your life

Choose your days off. Work blocks on these days will be flagged so you can move them.

Keep vacations and education events consistent with the working weeks in your Economic Model. This routine repeats during working weeks; it does not book every week of the year.

2. Shape your working week

Choose days and start/stop times. Turn off blocks you do not need or delegate. Each appointment window can hold multiple appointments.

Choose appointment days

Reserve the number needed by your Economic Model. Weekdays start at 5:30 PM; weekend appointments follow open houses with a 30-minute buffer. Changing these days or appointment length rebuilds appointment placeholders only.

Your Weekly Rhythm

My Timeblocked Schedule

Appointment blocks are placeholders. Drag a block to another time or day. Drag its bottom edge to resize. Changes snap to 15 minutes and affect only that day. Click a block to edit precise times. Leave unscheduled space for the unexpected.
