Build a Business That Funds Your Life by Design.

“The purpose of business is to fund your truly perfect life.”

This planning module builds a complete business plan that moves you from confusion to clarity about what your goals require and how to achieve them. Success is not easy, but it is usually simpler than most people make it. Start by calculating how much money it takes to fund your truly perfect life.

Here is what to expect

Each step turns a larger life or business goal into a number you can use. By the end, you will have an income target, a complete economic model, and a practical path from annual goals to the activity your calendar needs to contain.

  1. 1. Life by DesignPut a real number on the life you want—living costs, reserves, lifestyle, giving, future goals, and taxes.
  2. 2. Economic ModelTranslate that income target into GCI, expenses, volume, closings, agreements, contracts, and appointments.
  3. 3. Lead Generation ModelDetermine the lead-generation activity required to create the appointments in your Economic Model.
  4. 4. GPS Business PlanTurn the production goal into priorities and strategies you can execute.
  5. 5. Time Blocking & 411Put the activity into your calendar and turn annual goals into weekly commitments.
  6. 6. AccountabilityTrack goals versus actual results, identify gaps early, and adjust before the year gets away from you.
